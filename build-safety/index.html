Build Safety & Educational Disclaimer

The projects described in Seer Frights books and on this website are provided for educational purposes only.

Important Safety Information

  • Adult supervision is required.
  • Soldering must be performed with proper ventilation.
  • Coin batteries can cause serious internal injury if swallowed. Seek immediate medical attention if ingestion is suspected.
  • Do not connect projects to household mains electricity.
  • Disconnect power if components become hot or damaged.
