The Books

Each book. One town. One fae. One set of rules the town forgot.

Seer Fairies #1 · Published

The Púca

Hollow Creek, WV — an Appalachian crossroads town with no tradition of the Irish fae that has just arrived. A black horse with gold eyes that cannot lie. A thirteen-year-old boy carrying a truth about his father he has been refusing to say out loud for two years. The púca does not create truth. It removes the last wall between you and it.

Seer Fairies #4 · Published

The Sluagh

Dryden, KS — a dying town on the Great Plains where the west wind never stops and nobody knows to seal the west-facing windows. A flock of grey figures in the night sky, carrying the faces of the unmourned dead. A fourteen-year-old girl in her dead grandmother’s barn jacket. The host does not depart. The host settles.

Seer Fairies #5 · Published

The Each-Uisge

Millbrook, TN — a town drowned under a TVA reservoir, its church still standing on the lake bottom. The water horse waits in the deep water. First entity the Seer successfully destroys. Bring iron from the drowned church.

In Review The Dullahan

Seer Fairies #2 · In Review

The Dullahan

Ferris Bend, MN — a Mississippi bluff town with a condemned iron bridge nobody will close. The headless rider speaks one name per ride. Every barrier fails. Reverse the decision and the name goes unspoken.

In Writing The Leanan Sídhe

Seer Fairies #3 · In Writing

The Leanan Sídhe

Ashfield, VT — a covered-bridge town of dying arts. The kiss that produces genius and quietly drains the life that made it. The only defense is the one the subject discovers alone.

In Writing The Changeling

Seer Fairies #6 · In Writing

The Changeling

WV coal hollow — a boy who was taken home and is no longer the boy his mother knew. Earl, the mine rescue captain, still keeps the iron-and-eggshell test in his notebook.
