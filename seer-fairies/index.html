The Series

Ancient European fae are waking in American towns where the knowing that once managed them has been forgotten. Every fairy followed the immigrants who carried them across the Atlantic — inside the brickmaking, the mining, the hearth-keeping, the way grandmothers washed the dead.


The immigrants also brought the rules. Then the factories closed. The mines sealed. The grandmothers died without telling anyone. And the fae remained — still in the land, still inside the practice that no longer practiced — waking into a country with no defenses.


The Seer follows the waking. Every book is a case. Every case is a cost. Every book ends with field notes: what the entity is, how it picks you, how to spot it, how to survive it, and what it will cost you if you get it wrong.

The Core Thesis

The displacement is not the fae coming to America. The displacement is the compact leaving America.


The fae were brought inside the immigrants’ techniques, traditions, and industrial processes. The compact — the rules, the offerings, the management that kept the fae contained — was brought alongside. Then the techniques died. The compact broke. The fae remained.


The Seer is the last compact-keeper.

Seer Fairies is published by Institute for Seer LLC. Twenty-two standalone novels by B. Carter for upper middle grade and lower YA readers.
