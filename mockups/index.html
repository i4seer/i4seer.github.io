i4Seer homepage mockups

Three takes on the same brief: tech-first pivot, light theme, books secondary. Same content (Tick deployment tiers, transport modes, books strip). Different visual personality. Pick one to roll out site-wide.

Option A

Console

Operator-software aesthetic.

Looks and feels like the Tick dashboard itself. White surfaces, monospace headers, a live-data preview right in the hero. Books appear in a quiet sibling strip.

  • Strongest "we ship a product" signal
  • Closest to the live tick.i4seer.com aesthetic
  • Tabular density — lots of facts above the fold

Option B

Studio

Editorial / paper aesthetic.

Warm cream background, serif typography, magazine-style hero with a sidebar of key facts. Books get a warmer presence on a slightly tinted backdrop.

  • Reads as a serious independent studio, not a startup
  • Honors that you also publish literary work
  • Most distinctive personality — nothing else looks like this
Open Option B →
Option C

Field Manual

Engineering / blueprint aesthetic.

Subtle grid background, "sheet 01 / sheet 02" framing, FIG callouts and revision tags. Reads as a technical-spec brochure that happens to also be a website.

  • Strongest "we are engineers" signal
  • Most differentiated from typical SaaS landing pages
  • Books slot in cleanly as "Addendum A"
Open Option C →

Same content across all three:

  • Hero leads with Tick value prop ("Telemetry that ships anywhere")
  • Stats bar with the same four numbers (~30s first read, 3 transports, 3 destinations, $4/mo droplet)
  • Three deployment tiers (BYO backend / self-host ours / i4Seer-hosted) with mode 3 highlighted as default
  • Three transport modes (WiFi / cellular / WiFi+backup)
  • Books appear as a clearly-secondary section toward the bottom
  • Footer with Tick / Books / Company columns

What's different: typography, surface treatment, structural metaphor (dashboard vs. magazine vs. spec sheet), and how books are presented within the secondary slot.

What I haven't built yet: these are homepage-only sketches. Whichever you pick, the design system rolls out to /tick/, /tick/how-it-works/, /about/, /press/ and the four legal pages. Book series pages stay on their existing dark theme — horror needs to feel like horror.
