Do not sell or share my personal information.

Your California privacy rights, and what we don’t do with your data.

We do not sell or share your personal information

Institute for Seer, LLC (d/b/a i4Seer) does not sell your personal information to third parties. We do not share your personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising.

This applies to all visitors, including California residents under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) and the California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA).

Your rights

Under California law, you have the right to:

  • Know what personal information we collect, use, and disclose.
  • Request deletion of your personal information.
  • Request correction of inaccurate personal information.
  • Opt out of the sale or sharing of your personal information. Because we do not sell or share personal information, no opt-out action is required.
  • Non-discrimination for exercising your privacy rights.

How to contact us

If you have questions about our data practices or wish to exercise any of your privacy rights, contact:

Institute for Seer, LLC (d/b/a i4Seer)
Email: privacy@i4seer.com

We will verify your identity before processing your request and respond within 45 days.

For full details, see our Privacy Policy, Section 9 (Notice to California Residents).
