The device.

A small, rugged unit that lives where the equipment lives. Plug it in, point it at the dashboard you choose, and it starts reporting in seconds.

Plug-and-play

~30 sec to first reading

Power on, network attach, first telemetry post — the whole cold-start path takes about half a minute. No router config, no SIM activation paperwork.

On-device screen

Diagnostics in your hand

0.85" IPS display shows transport state, signal strength, and last-send age. Press the button to cycle views. Field-friendly without an app.

Hardware-rooted ID

Identity that survives anything

Every device’s ID is derived from its hardware. Reflash, factory-reset, swap the SIM — the same physical unit is the same identity, every time.

Telemetry data.

Tick stores every reading. Routes forward that same data to wherever you actually use it — a dashboard, an alerting system, an in-house pipeline.

Store

Persisted, queryable

Every reading lands in the portal with full context: which transport carried it, round-trip time, device health, and the rolling-window summary. Searchable by device, fleet, or time range.

Sense often

Lo / hi / avg / last per window

Sample every 30 seconds, send every 30 minutes — the report carries the full window. Catch short events without paying for thousands of redundant data points.

Routes Coming

Forward to your stack

The portal pushes events out as they arrive: HTTPS first, with MQTT, AWS, Azure, Google Cloud, Twilio, Datacake, Blynk, and Qubitro in development.

The portal.

Where your fleet lives. Sign in, see every device, drill into any one of them — including a live remote terminal session, from any browser.

Remote console

Reach any device, live

Open a terminal session through the dashboard. Sessions go live in seconds. Multi-admin sessions, full transcript saved to audit, transport-aware acceleration.

Multi-tenant

Orgs · fleets · roles

Real org / fleet / role model from day one. Inspectors with cross-customer access. Contractors locked to a single fleet. Platform admins separated from regular users.

Audit

Every action signed and logged

Approvals, label changes, console sessions, downlinks — all recorded with operator identity. Members read their own org’s history; platform admins see global events.

Pick the transport.

WiFi for connected sites. Cellular for places without IT. Both when downtime hurts.

Transport 01

WiFi only

For offices, kitchens, warehouses with reliable WiFi. No cellular cost, no SIM. The fastest transport.

Transport 02

Cellular only

For rentals, jobsites, mobile units, remote outbuildings. Global SIM, nationwide carrier coverage. Plug in anywhere.

Transport 03

WiFi + cellular backup

WiFi to save cost; cellular kicks in instantly when WiFi blips. An outage can’t take you offline.

Who Tick is for.

Anyone with expensive equipment in places without IT staff or reliable WiFi.

Cold-Chain

Restaurants · Pharma · Food trucks

Restaurant freezer monitoring. Pharma cold-chain. Mobile units. The “freezer #4 dropped to 41°F” story. Devices ride along anywhere there’s cell signal.

Field Service

HVAC · Rentals · Generators

Equipment rental fleets, jobsite generators, HVAC installations. Customer wants to know if a unit is running without sending a tech to check.

Inspectors

One identity, many customers

Contractors and inspectors with cross-customer access. The org / fleet / role model lets one user see fleet A in customer X and fleet B in customer Y, scoped exactly.

Enterprise

Compliance-bound operations

Procurement requirements, data-residency rules, sector-specific compliance. The Tick Private Portal deployment puts your data and your portal under your control, with paid setup and support.
