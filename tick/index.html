Pick the transport per site.

WiFi for connected sites. Cellular for places without IT. Both when downtime hurts.

Transport 01

WiFi only

For offices, kitchens, warehouses with reliable WiFi. No cellular cost, no SIM. The fastest transport.

Transport 02

Cellular only

For rentals, jobsites, mobile units, remote outbuildings. Global SIM, nationwide carrier coverage. Plug in anywhere.

Transport 03

WiFi + cellular backup

WiFi to save cost; cellular kicks in instantly when WiFi blips. An outage can’t take you offline.

Operate from anywhere.

Tick’s remote console lets you reach any device through your dashboard — from a browser, in real time. The same operator experience as standing in front of the unit with a USB cable.

// real-time

Open a session in seconds.

Click Open Session. Pick the device. Type. Sessions go live in about two seconds on WiFi, five on cellular. Live transcript, ANSI colors, scrollback — everything you’d expect from a real terminal.

// multi-operator

More than one admin can attend.

Several admins can attach to the same session at the same time. They see each other’s commands and the device’s responses live. No “take over from Alice” choreography. Every command is signed by the operator who issued it.

// transport-aware

Choose the route per session.

Open the session on WiFi to save cellular minutes; switch to cellular mid-session if WiFi drops. The chosen transport accelerates for responsiveness; the other stays at idle.

// auto-end

Walk away safely.

Sessions end automatically two minutes after the last activity from either side. The full transcript is saved server-side, visible later from the audit log. No leaked sessions, no forgotten terminals.

Routes. Coming

Tick devices send data in to your portal. Routes send data out — events forwarded to your destinations as they arrive. Same JSON the device speaks, signed the same way.

The portal becomes the routing layer for your fleet’s telemetry. Forward to a custom HTTPS endpoint, an MQTT broker, a cloud queue, an SMS gateway, an IoT dashboard — or anywhere else you can describe.

Who Tick is for.

Anyone with expensive equipment in places without IT staff or reliable WiFi.

Cold-Chain

Restaurants · Pharma · Food trucks

Restaurant freezer monitoring. Pharma cold-chain. Mobile units. The “freezer #4 dropped to 41°F” story. Devices ride along anywhere there’s cell signal.

Field Service

HVAC · Rentals · Generators

Equipment rental fleets, jobsite generators, HVAC installations. Customer wants to know if a unit is running without sending a tech to check.

Inspectors

One identity, many customers

Contractors and inspectors with cross-customer access. The org / fleet / role model lets one user see fleet A in customer X and fleet B in customer Y, scoped exactly.

Enterprise

Compliance-bound operations

Procurement requirements, data-residency rules, sector-specific compliance. The Tick Private Portal deployment puts your data and your portal under your control, with paid setup and support.
