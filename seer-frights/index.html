SEER FRIGHTS

Horror for readers who like their scares smart, their mysteries real, and their devices homemade. No gore. No nightmares. Just the good kind of scared.

Middle-grade horror. Every book drops a group into an ordinary place that turns wrong — smart scares, real mysteries, and a device the villain built that readers can actually make.
