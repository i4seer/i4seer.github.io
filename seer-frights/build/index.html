Build the thing that scared you.

Every Seer Frights book features a device the kids in the story put together — a swamp glow, a bus tracker, a doorframe listener, a locker tap. Each one really works. The build pages walk you through parts, wiring, code, and what to expect when you flip the switch.

The builds.

Listed in publication order. Difficulty grows gradually — pick the book you’ve read.

💡
From: Don’t Go Into the Swamp

The Swamp Glow

A waterproof bobbing light kit. Microcontroller, RGB LED, sealed enclosure, USB-C battery. Drift it across a pond at night. Watch a friend’s face.

 🚇

The Bus Tracker

A small GPS module that logs movement to a microSD. Stick it under a school bus seat (or your dad’s commute). Watch the trail show up on a phone map.

 🔊

The Three-Knock Listener

The Locker Tap

A vibration sensor with a magnetic mount and a tiny OLED. Stick it on a metal locker. Watch the hum graph itself in real time.

Don’t want to source the parts? Pre-bundled kits are in production. Each kit ships with the exact parts called out in the build page, a one-page wiring card, and a USB-C drive with the firmware preloaded. Email sales to be notified when kits go live.

The Swamp Glow.

From Don’t Go Into the Swamp. The first build in the series, and the gentlest one to start with.

A small microcontroller drives a chain of three RGB LEDs. The whole assembly slips inside a sealed plastic float. Battery powered, magnetic on/off switch, water-tested down to a meter.

Difficulty: Easy. No soldering required for the basic kit. Adult supervision recommended for water-testing.

Parts list, wiring diagram, and code download are coming with the next site update. Notify me when ready.

The Bus Tracker.

From Don’t Ride Route 13. A simple GPS logger you can stick somewhere and forget.

A microcontroller, a u-blox GPS module, and a microSD card. Logs latitude, longitude, and timestamp every five seconds. Pop the SD card into a laptop, drop the file on a map viewer, see the route.

Difficulty: Moderate. Light soldering required. A magnetic mount kit is included with the bundle.

Parts list and code download coming. Notify me when ready.

The Three-Knock Listener.

From Don’t Open the Door. The build that taught the kids the door wasn’t the only thing knocking.

A small microphone, a microcontroller with audio capture, and a 24-hour rolling buffer on flash. Press the front button to bookmark a moment. Plug into a laptop later to replay.

Difficulty: Moderate. Soldering, microphone calibration, and a wood-mounted enclosure if you want to embed it in a doorframe.

Parts list and code download coming. Notify me when ready.

The Locker Tap.

From Don’t Open the Locker. A vibration listener with a tiny screen.

An accelerometer-based vibration sensor on a magnetic mount, with a 0.96" OLED that graphs the signal in real time. Stick it on any metal panel. Watch the hum draw itself.

Difficulty: Moderate. Soldering and a 3D-printed mount (STL file included).

Parts list, STL, and code download coming. Notify me when ready.
