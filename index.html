Seer Warns

“You were warned.”

Twenty standalone novels, one small American town. A stranger sits down, names the exact cost of the choice you are about to make, and leaves. You believe every word. You make the choice anyway. Four books out now.

Seer Frights

“You should be scared.”

Standalone scares for kids who finished Goosebumps and want more of exactly that. Every chapter ends on a scare. Half of them are fake — and you never know which until you turn the page. Five books out now.

For authors.

More voices, standalone titles. We’re open to other independent authors working in literary and dark fiction.

Seer Press offers publishing, editorial review, cover and interior design, marketing and press support, and a long view on series planning. We publish series, not one-offs — we plan with you for book two before book one ships.

Send sample chapters, a project description, and a sense of the series arc to authors@i4seer.com.
