About i4seer

Institute for Seer is an independent publisher and technology studio. Right now we’re building two things: stories that make kids look twice at the world around them, and devices that watch the world so you don’t have to. Every book has a mystery, every mystery has a device, and every device actually works. The stories come first. The tech follows.


Seer Frights is our middle-grade horror series by B. Carter. Three books so far. Each one drops kids into an ordinary place that turns wrong — smart scares, real mysteries, and a device the villain built that readers can actually make.


Tick is the next step. Real IoT sensing devices and code you can read. Start with the projects from the books — then take them anywhere.


Different projects. Same instinct: pay attention to what’s actually happening.
