About i4Seer

Institute for Seer is an independent publisher and technology studio. Right now we’re building two things: stories that make readers look twice at the world around them, and devices that watch the world so you don’t have to. Every book has a mystery, every mystery has a device, and every device actually works. The stories come first. The tech follows.


Seer Frights is our middle-grade horror series by B. Carter. Every book drops a group into an ordinary place that turns wrong — smart scares, real mysteries, and a device the villain built that readers can actually make.


Seer Warns is our adult literary horror series. Standalone novels about ordinary people who receive warnings so precise they cannot be doubted — and walk into the fire anyway.


Seer Fairies is our dark fae horror series for upper middle grade and lower YA. Ancient European fae are waking in American towns where the knowing that once managed them has been forgotten.


SEER Tick is our IoT monitoring platform. Rugged LoRaWAN sensors, private gateways, a secure cloud console, and email & Teams alerts at tick.i4seer.com. Built for operators who want focused simplicity instead of bloated commercial suites.


Different projects. Same instinct: pay attention to what’s actually happening.
