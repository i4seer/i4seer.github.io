The press

Small, independent, and built around finished books.

Seer Press is the book imprint of Institute for Seer LLC, based in the United States. The company is built around books, the authors who write them, and the audiences who find them—publishing first, with series sites, press work, and other media growing from the catalog.

We publish original fiction across different audiences and tones. Each series is given its own visual system and dedicated site so the publisher can grow without forcing every story into the same template.

Author · current roster

B. Carter

B. Carter writes across fantasy and horror, building stories around strange rules, impossible situations, and the people who have to live with them.

A Native American computer engineer and veteran with a PhD in Computer Engineering and Computer Science, he brings a builder’s curiosity to every story: How does this world work? What happens when one rule changes? What would a real person do next?
