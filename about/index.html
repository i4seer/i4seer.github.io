The press

Many stories.
One independent press.

Seer Press is the publishing imprint of Institute for Seer LLC. Its catalog spans fantasy, adventure, folklore, horror, mystery, and suspense.

Each collection has a character of its own. Seer Worlds follows a curious traveler through impossible places. Journey grows with a boy and his map. Frights and Fairies find danger close to home. Warns follows the consequences of a choice. Stories opens a different life with every book.

The dedicated sites give those books room to be themselves. Here, you can discover the collections, meet the author, and find the press.

In the author’s words

About the author

I write the kind of books I enjoy — full of wonder, big questions, and just enough answers to keep the pages turning.

When I’m not writing, I’m usually reading, walking, or staring at the sky, letting a new idea take shape.

I live with too many books, not enough shelves, and a strong policy of leaving the hall light on.

Thanks for joining the journey. There’s always more to see.

B. Carter
