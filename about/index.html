The press

Small, independent, and built around stories that have somewhere to go.

Seer Press is the book imprint of Institute for Seer LLC, based in the United States. The company is built around books, the authors who write them, and the audiences who find them—publishing first, with series sites, press work, and other media growing from the catalog.

We publish independent authors and design titles to work as complete reading experiences. Seer Worlds has an ongoing larger journey, while Seer Frights and Seer Warns are built as standalone entries; each series is presented so a reader can understand where to begin.

The current catalog spans three tones: the adventurous mystery of Seer Worlds, the creepy fun of Seer Frights, and the quieter moral horror of Seer Warns.

Author · current roster

B. Carter

B. Carter is the author behind Seer Worlds, Seer Frights, and Seer Warns—three series that explore wonder, fear, strange rules, and the choices people make when reality stops behaving.

A Native American computer engineer and veteran with a PhD in Computer Engineering and Computer Science, he brings a builder’s curiosity to every story: How does this world work? What happens when one rule changes? What would a real person do next?
