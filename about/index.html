i4Seer is Institute for Seer LLC — a small studio that ships hardware and publishes books. Tick is the product line. Institute for Seer Press is the imprint. Same instinct: pay attention to what’s actually happening.

The company.

Two halves of one studio. Hardware on one side, fiction on the other.

Institute for Seer LLC is an independent technology studio based in the United States. Our primary product line is Tick, a plug-and-play telemetry platform for distributed equipment. We also operate Institute for Seer Press, an imprint publishing horror, dark fiction, and folklore-adjacent literary work.

The two halves share an instinct: pay attention to what’s actually happening. Our hardware puts eyes on equipment that’s never been watched. Our fiction is about people who notice something they aren’t supposed to notice. Different outputs, same reflex.

We design, write, build, and operate from the United States. Everything you see on this site is ours — the firmware, the dashboard, the stories, the cover art, the copy. We don’t license. We don’t white-label. We ship under our own name.

Tick.

Our hardware product. Cellular and WiFi telemetry, multi-tenant from day one, deploy anywhere with cell coverage.

A small device that monitors distributed equipment and reports to whatever dashboard you choose. WiFi for connected sites, cellular for places without IT, both for redundancy. Send data to your existing backend, a Tick Private Portal deployment for enterprise, or the i4Seer-hosted portal.

Live at tick.i4seer.com. Public read-only fleet at /public.

Author

B. Carter

Seer Frights · Seer Fairies · Seer Warns

Native American computer engineer, veteran, rock collector, hiker, and lifelong reader of adventure books, manga, and comics. PhD in Computer Engineering and Computer Science.

The Seer Frights series started from a simple thought: the best scary stories are the ones where you can build the thing that scared you. The Tick platform grew out of that hardware research.
