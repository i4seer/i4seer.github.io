Seer Warns

“You were warned.”

Twenty standalone novels, one small American town. A stranger with still hands sits down across from an ordinary person, names the exact cost of the choice they are about to make, and leaves. Every protagonist believes the warning. Every protagonist makes the choice anyway. Four out now.

Seer Frights

“You should be scared.”

Standalone scary books for kids who finished Goosebumps and want more of exactly that — and for the grown-ups who grew up on it. Every chapter ends on a scare. Half of them are fake. The other half are real. Five out now.
