The Author

B. Carter writes all three i4Seer book series. Native American computer engineer, veteran, rock collector, hiker, and lifelong reader of adventure books, manga, and comics. PhD in Computer Engineering and Computer Science. When he is not writing, he is hiking with a backpack full of sensors, seeing what happens when circuits meet the real world.


The Seer Frights series started because he thought the best scary stories are the ones where you can build the thing that scared you. The SEER Tick IoT platform grew out of that hardware research.

Where to Start

If You Like Smart Scares

Start with Don’t Go Into the Swamp. Three kids, brand-new shoes, one very bad idea. The opening of Seer Frights.

If You Want Folklore Horror

Start with The Púca. Hollow Creek, WV. A black horse with gold eyes that cannot lie. The opening of Seer Fairies.

If You Read Literary Horror

Start with The Good Father. A history teacher. A promise. A spreadsheet that makes the math work. Until it doesn’t.

Every i4Seer book is standalone. No reading order required across or within series. Pick the one whose hook lands hardest and start there.
