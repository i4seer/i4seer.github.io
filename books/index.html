Where to start.

Three doorways into the same imprint. Pick the audience or the appetite.

If you like smart scares

Don’t Go Into the Swamp

Three kids, brand-new shoes, one very bad idea. The opening of Seer Frights.

If you want folklore horror

The Púca

Hollow Creek, WV. A black horse with gold eyes that cannot lie. The opening of Seer Fairies.

If you read literary horror

The Good Father

A history teacher. A promise. A spreadsheet that makes the math work. Until it doesn’t. The opening of Seer Warns.

For authors.

More series, more voices. We’re open to other authors working in horror, dark fiction, and folklore-adjacent literary work.

Institute for Seer Press offers publishing, editorial review, cover and interior design, marketing and press support, and a long view on series planning. We publish series, not one-offs — we plan with you for book two before book one ships.

Send sample chapters, a project description, and a sense of the series arc to authors@i4seer.com.
