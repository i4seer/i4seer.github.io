Don't Miss the Bus

The 7:15 bus. Route 13. Two kids. One wrong turn.


The streets outside don't have signs. The other passengers aren't quite right. And the driver won't stop.


Every Seer Frights book drops kids into an ordinary place that turns wrong. This time it's a city bus. This time there's no trail to follow back. This time — you can't just walk away.

What We Know So Far

Two kids get on a city bus after dark. The route is familiar. The driver is not.


The bus makes a turn that isn't on the map. The street signs disappear. The other passengers sit too still, smile too wide, and never look out the windows.


One kid wants to pull the cord and get off. The other says wait. The bus keeps moving. The doors stay closed.


And the driver — the driver just keeps driving.
