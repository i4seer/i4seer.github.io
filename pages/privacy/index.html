Privacy Policy

Effective Date: December 1, 2025

1. Introduction

Institute for Seer, LLC, doing business as i4Seer ("i4Seer," "we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard information when you visit our website, use our products (including the Tick sensor module), or access our cloud services (Twin API).

By accessing or using our services, you agree to this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use our services.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide:

  • Contact information (name, email address, phone number, company name)
  • Account credentials for cloud services
  • Payment and billing information
  • Communications you send to us (support requests, feedback)
  • Order and shipping information

2.2 Information Collected Automatically:

  • Device identifiers (MAC address, device ID)
  • Sensor readings and telemetry data transmitted to our cloud services
  • Device logs, connection timestamps, and network metadata
  • IP address and approximate geographic location
  • Browser type, operating system, and device information
  • Website usage data (pages visited, time spent, referring URLs)

2.3 Cookies and Tracking Technologies:

We use cookies, web beacons, and similar technologies to enhance your experience, analyze usage patterns, and remember your preferences. See Section 8 for details.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve our products and services
  • Process orders and deliver products
  • Operate our cloud services and store your sensor data
  • Respond to your inquiries and provide customer support
  • Send transactional emails (order confirmations, service updates)
  • Send marketing communications (with your consent)
  • Analyze usage patterns to improve our offerings
  • Detect, prevent, and address security issues and fraud
  • Comply with legal obligations

4. Disclosure of Information

We may share your information with:

  • Service Providers: Third parties who perform services on our behalf (cloud hosting, payment processing, shipping, analytics)
  • Business Partners: With your consent, for joint marketing or product offerings
  • Legal Requirements: When required by law, court order, or governmental authority
  • Business Transfers: In connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets
  • Protection of Rights: To protect our rights, property, safety, or that of others

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

5. Data Storage and Security

5.1 Storage: Your data is stored on servers located in the United States, primarily using Microsoft Azure infrastructure. By using our services, you consent to the transfer and storage of your information in the United States.

5.2 Security: We implement industry-standard security measures including encryption in transit (TLS) and at rest, access controls, and regular security assessments. However, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

5.3 Retention: We retain your information for as long as necessary to provide our services, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements. Sensor data in our cloud services is retained according to your account settings or until you delete it.

IMPORTANT: You are solely responsible for the data you collect, store, and transmit using i4Seer products and services. This includes:

7. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the following rights:

  • Access: Request a copy of the personal information we hold about you
  • Correction: Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your personal information

  • Withdraw Consent: Withdraw previously given consent

To exercise these rights, contact us at hello@i4seer.com. We will respond within 30 days.

8. Cookies and Tracking

8.1 Types of Cookies We Use:

  • Essential Cookies: Required for basic website functionality

Our services are operated from the United States. If you access our services from outside the United States, your information will be transferred to, stored, and processed in the United States where data protection laws may differ from your jurisdiction.
