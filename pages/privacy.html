Privacy Policy

Last updated: December 2025

Information We Collect

Contact Information: When you contact us via email or our website, we collect your name, email address, and any information you choose to provide.

Device Data: If you use our Twin cloud service, we collect sensor readings, device identifiers, and connection metadata to provide the service.

Website Analytics: We use cookies and similar technologies to understand how visitors use our website, including pages visited, time spent, and referring sources.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Respond to your inquiries and provide customer support
  • Deliver and maintain our products and services
  • Improve our website and product offerings
  • Send product updates and service announcements
  • Comply with legal obligations

Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely using industry-standard encryption. Cloud service data is hosted on Microsoft Azure infrastructure in the United States. We retain data only as long as necessary to provide our services or as required by law.

Third-Party Services

We may use third-party services for analytics, email, and cloud infrastructure. These providers have their own privacy policies and are contractually obligated to protect your data.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Opt out of marketing communications
  • Withdraw consent for data processing

Cookies

Our website uses cookies to remember your preferences and analyze site traffic. You can control cookies through your browser settings. Declining cookies may limit some website functionality.
