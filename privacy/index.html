Privacy Policy

Effective Date: February 27, 2026

1. Introduction

Institute for Seer, LLC, doing business as i4Seer ("i4Seer," "we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard information when you visit our website (i4Seer.com), read our publications (including the Seer Frights book series), use our products (including Tick sensor devices and build-it project guides), or access our cloud services (Twin API).

By accessing or using our services, you agree to this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use our services.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide:

  • Contact information (name, email address, phone number)
  • Email addresses (if voluntarily submitted)
  • Account credentials for cloud services
  • Payment and billing information
  • Communications you send to us (support requests, feedback)
  • Order and shipping information
  • Purchase information (if applicable)

2.2 Information Collected Automatically:

  • Device identifiers (MAC address, device ID) from Tick sensor devices
  • Sensor readings and telemetry data transmitted to our cloud services
  • Device logs, connection timestamps, and network metadata
  • IP address and approximate geographic location
  • Browser type, operating system, and device information
  • Website usage data (pages visited, time spent, referring URLs)
  • Basic analytics data

2.3 Cookies and Tracking Technologies:

We use cookies, web beacons, and similar technologies to enhance your experience, analyze usage patterns, and remember your preferences. See Section 10 for details.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve our products and services
  • Process orders and deliver products
  • Operate our cloud services and store your sensor data
  • Respond to your inquiries and provide customer support
  • Send transactional emails (order confirmations, service updates)
  • Send marketing communications (with your consent)
  • Analyze usage patterns to improve our offerings
  • Detect, prevent, and address security issues and fraud
  • Comply with legal obligations

4. Disclosure of Information

We may share your information with:

  • Service Providers: Third parties who perform services on our behalf (cloud hosting, payment processing, shipping, analytics)
  • Business Partners: With your consent, for joint marketing or product offerings
  • Legal Requirements: When required by law, court order, or governmental authority
  • Business Transfers: In connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets
  • Protection of Rights: To protect our rights, property, safety, or that of others

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

5. Data Storage and Security

5.1 Storage: Your data is stored on servers located in the United States, primarily using Microsoft Azure infrastructure. By using our services, you consent to the transfer and storage of your information in the United States.

5.2 Security: We implement industry-standard security measures including encryption in transit (TLS) and at rest, access controls, and regular security assessments. However, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

5.3 Retention: We retain your information according to the following guidelines:

  • Account data: Retained for the life of your account and up to 30 days after deletion request.
  • Order and purchase records: Retained for 7 years to comply with tax and accounting obligations.
  • Cloud sensor data: Retained according to your account settings or until you delete it.
  • Website analytics: Aggregated and anonymized within 26 months of collection.
  • Support communications: Retained for 3 years after resolution.
  • Cookie consent preferences: Stored locally in your browser until you clear your data.

When data is no longer needed for any of the above purposes, we securely delete or anonymize it.

  • Consent: Where you have given clear consent for us to process your data for a specific purpose (e.g., marketing emails, cookie preferences).
  • Contract: Where processing is necessary to fulfill a contract with you (e.g., processing an order, providing cloud services).
  • Legitimate Interests: Where processing is necessary for our legitimate business interests (e.g., website analytics, fraud prevention, improving our services), provided those interests are not overridden by your rights.
  • Legal Obligation: Where processing is necessary to comply with a legal requirement.

8.2 Your GDPR Rights: In addition to the rights listed in Section 7, you have the right to:

  • Restriction: Request that we restrict the processing of your personal data in certain circumstances.

To safeguard your data during international transfers, we rely on one or more of the following mechanisms as appropriate: (a) Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) approved by the European Commission, incorporated into our agreements with service providers; (b) your explicit consent to the transfer, which you may withdraw at any time by contacting us (though this may affect our ability to provide services to you). Where we engage sub-processors who access EEA personal data, we ensure equivalent protections are in place.

8.4 Supervisory Authority: You have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection supervisory authority if you believe we have not complied with applicable data protection laws.

8.5 Data Protection Contact: For GDPR-related inquiries, contact us at seer@i4seer.com.

9.3 Your CCPA/CPRA Rights: As a California resident, you have the right to:

  • Know: Request what personal information we have collected, used, disclosed, or sold about you.
  • Delete: Request deletion of your personal information, subject to certain exceptions.

9.4 How to Submit a Request: To exercise your CCPA/CPRA rights, contact us at seer@i4seer.com. We will verify your identity before processing your request and respond within 45 days.
