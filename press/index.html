Publisher boilerplate

Seer Press in one paragraph.

Seer Press is the independent publishing imprint of Institute for Seer LLC. Its current catalog includes three series by B. Carter: Seer Worlds, fantasy adventures built around impossible rules and an ongoing mystery; Seer Frights, spooky-fun middle-grade horror for ages 8 and up; and Seer Warns, standalone adult literary horror set in the town of Harmon. The press develops books from manuscript through cover, interior, dedicated series sites, and press support.

Series boilerplate

Three distinct series.

Seer Worlds

A fantasy adventure series about a small traveler called the Dot crossing worlds where the rules work differently. Each book resolves a local story while clues about the Dot and the crossings build across the larger journey. Nine books currently form the first arc.

seerworlds.com ↗

Seer Frights

Middle-grade horror for ages 8 and up: standalone scary stories full of eerie places, dangerous objects, fake-outs, and real scares. Creepy and fast-moving without gore. Five books are represented in the current catalog.

seerfrights.com ↗

Seer Warns

A planned twenty-book series of standalone adult literary horror novels set in Harmon. In each, an ordinary person receives a warning precise enough to be believed—and then chooses what to do with it. Four novels are published in the current catalog.

seerwarns.com ↗

Author bio

B. Carter

B. Carter is the author of Seer Worlds, Seer Frights, and Seer Warns. A Native American computer engineer and veteran with a PhD in Computer Engineering and Computer Science, he writes stories about ordinary people facing strange rules, impossible choices, and worlds that never work quite the way they expect.

His work ranges from the adventurous mystery of Seer Worlds, to the creepy fun of Seer Frights, to the quieter moral horror of Seer Warns.
