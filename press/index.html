100 words

Institute for Seer LLC (i4Seer) is an independent technology studio. Our primary product is Tick, a plug-and-play telemetry platform: a credit-card-sized device sends signed JSON to whatever endpoint a customer chooses — their own backend, a Tick Private Portal enterprise deployment, or the i4Seer-hosted portal. WiFi, cellular, or both. An operator-grade browser UI manages devices end-to-end — queued remote commands, firmware OTA, signed audit, structured on-device error log. We also operate Institute for Seer Press, an imprint publishing the Seer Warns horror series by author B. Carter and open to other voices in horror, dark fiction, and folklore-adjacent literary work. Books and hardware share one instinct: pay attention to what is actually happening.

Short bio

B. Carter is a Native American computer engineer, veteran, and horror writer. He holds a PhD in Computer Engineering and Computer Science. He writes the Seer Frights, Seer Fairies, and Seer Warns series for Institute for Seer LLC.

Long bio

B. Carter is a Native American computer engineer, veteran, rock collector, hiker, and lifelong reader of adventure books, manga, and comics. He holds a PhD in Computer Engineering and Computer Science. When he is not writing, he is hiking with a backpack full of sensors, seeing what happens when circuits meet the real world. His Seer Frights middle-grade horror series started because he thought the best scary stories are the ones where you can build the thing that scared you. His Seer Warns adult literary series is about ordinary people who receive warnings so precise they cannot be doubted. His Seer Fairies series is about ancient European fae waking in American towns where the rules have been forgotten. He helps shape Tick, the IoT product that grew out of the hardware in the books.

Tick boilerplate.

For coverage focused on the hardware product specifically.

Tick is an IoT telemetry platform built by i4Seer. A tiny device with a global cellular SIM ships HMAC-signed JSON to any HTTPS endpoint within seconds of power-on. Three transport modes (WiFi only, cellular only, or WiFi with cellular backup) and three deployment modes (a customer’s existing backend, a Tick Private Portal enterprise deployment, or the i4Seer-hosted portal) — configurable per device.

Live portal at tick.i4seer.com with an operator-grade UI over a live WebSocket: per-device panel with transport indicator, queued remote commands, firmware OTA with chunked download and resume, signed audit log, and a structured on-device error log that surfaces in the dashboard. End-customer accounts with org / fleet / role separation are next.

Trademark guidelines.

When referring to our products in articles, reviews, or announcements.

i4Seer™ — preferred over “I4Seer” or “i4seer”. The “i” is always lowercase; the “4” is a numeral; “Seer” is capitalized.

Tick™ or Tick by i4Seer™ — the telemetry platform. “Tick” is the product name; “Tick by i4Seer” in formal first reference.

Seer Frights® — registered mark; use the ® on first reference in formal press materials.

Seer Warns™ and Seer Fairies™ — both two words, both capitalized. Spelled “Fairies,” not “Faeries.”

Institute for Seer LLC — the legal entity. Use on first reference in formal materials.

Do not alter logos, recolor marks, or combine them with other company names without written permission. For any commercial use, contact sales@i4seer.com.
