Publisher boilerplate

Seer Press in one paragraph.

Seer Press is the independent publishing imprint of Institute for Seer LLC. Its catalog spans fantasy, middle-grade horror, literary horror, and other original fiction published through distinctive series with dedicated reader experiences. The press develops books from manuscript through editorial, cover and interior design, production, series presentation, and press support.

Author bio

B. Carter

B. Carter writes across fantasy and horror, building stories around strange rules, impossible situations, and the people who have to live with them. A Native American computer engineer and veteran with a PhD in Computer Engineering and Computer Science, he brings a builder’s curiosity to every story.

Naming guidance

Use the names as shown.

Seer Press™ — the publishing imprint. Two words, both capitalized.

Seer Frights® — registered mark; use the ® on first reference in formal press materials.

Seer Warns™ — two words, both capitalized.

Seer Worlds — two words, both capitalized.

i4Seer™ — lowercase “i”, numeral “4”, capitalized “Seer”.

Institute for Seer LLC — the legal entity that operates Seer Press.
