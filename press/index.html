SHORT BIO

B. Carter is a Native American computer engineer, veteran, and horror writer. He holds a PhD in Computer Engineering and Computer Science. He writes the Seer Frights, Seer Warns, and Seer Fairies series for Institute for Seer LLC.

LONG BIO

B. Carter is a Native American computer engineer, veteran, rock collector, hiker, and lifelong reader of adventure books, manga, and comics. He holds a PhD in Computer Engineering and Computer Science. When he is not writing, he is hiking with a backpack full of sensors, seeing what happens when circuits meet the real world. His Seer Frights middle-grade horror series started because he thought the best scary stories are the ones where you can build the thing that scared you. His Seer Warns adult literary series is about ordinary people who receive warnings so precise they cannot be doubted. His Seer Fairies series is about ancient European fae waking in American towns where the rules have been forgotten. He also builds SEER Tick, the IoT platform that grew out of the hardware in the books.

SEER Tick Platform

SEER Tick is i4Seer’s IoT monitoring platform. Rugged LoRaWAN sensors. Private gateways. Secure cloud console. Microsoft and Google sign-in. Fleet-scoped RBAC. Email and Microsoft Teams alerts with quiet hours. Bearer tokens for Grafana and Power BI. Rate-limited ingest. Full audit with CSV export.


The platform was born out of the hardware research for the Seer Frights series and grew into a standalone product for operators who need focused simplicity rather than bloated commercial suites. Live portal at tick.i4seer.com.

Trademark Guidelines

When referring to our products in articles, reviews, or announcements:


  • › i4Seer™ — preferred over “I4Seer” or “i4seer”. The “i” is always lowercase; the “4” is a numeral; “Seer” is capitalized.
  • › Seer Frights® — registered mark; use the ® on first reference in formal press materials.
  • › Seer Warns™ — two words, both capitalized.
  • › Seer Fairies™ — two words, both capitalized. Spelled “Fairies,” not “Faeries.”
  • › SEER Tick™ or Tick by i4Seer™ — the hardware platform. “SEER Tick” in branding contexts, “Tick” fine in context.
  • › Institute for Seer LLC — the legal entity. Use on first reference in formal materials.

Do not alter logos, recolor marks, or combine them with other company names without written permission. For any commercial use, contact sales@i4seer.com.
