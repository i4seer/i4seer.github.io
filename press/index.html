Seer Press is an independent book imprint operated by Institute for Seer LLC. It publishes independent authors writing standalone titles in literary and dark fiction — every book designed to be read on its own, in any order. Its two series are by author B. Carter. Seer Warns is a twenty-novel literary psychological drama set in one small American town, where a stranger names the exact cost of a choice and the protagonist makes it anyway; four are published. Seer Frights is a Goosebumps-style middle-grade horror series for ages 8+; five are published. Both share one instinct: pay attention to what is actually happening.

Author: B. Carter.

Currently the only author publishing under Seer Press.

Short bio

B. Carter is a Native American computer engineer, veteran, and novelist. He holds a PhD in Computer Engineering and Computer Science. He writes the Seer Warns literary psychological drama series and the Seer Frights middle-grade horror series, both published by Seer Press.

Long bio

B. Carter is a Native American computer engineer, veteran, rock collector, hiker, and lifelong reader of adventure books, manga, and comics. He holds a PhD in Computer Engineering and Computer Science. When he is not writing, he is hiking with a backpack full of sensors, seeing what happens when circuits meet the real world. His Seer Warns adult literary series is about ordinary people who receive warnings so precise they cannot be doubted — and walk into the fire anyway; the series world lives at seerwarns.com. His Seer Frights series writes the same instinct for readers aged 8 and up, at seerfrights.com.

Series boilerplate.

For coverage focused on one series specifically.

Seer Warns is a series of twenty standalone literary psychological dramas set in one small American town. In each, a stranger with still hands sits down across from an ordinary person, names the exact cost of the choice they are about to make, and leaves. Every protagonist believes the warning, because the stranger knows the one thing only they could know. Every protagonist makes the choice anyway. No monster, no gore — the dread is in the arithmetic. Four novels are published; the rest are in review, in writing, or outlined. Series site: seerwarns.com.

Seer Frights is a Goosebumps-style middle-grade horror series for ages 8 and up — standalone scary books for kids who finished Goosebumps and want more of exactly that, and for the grown-ups who grew up on it. Every chapter ends on a scare; half are fake and half are real, and the reader never knows which until the page turns. Creepy, funny, bloodless, readable in any order. Five books are published. Series site: seerfrights.com.
