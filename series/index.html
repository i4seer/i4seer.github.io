Middle-grade fantasy

Seer Worlds

Different worlds. Same curious Dot.

A small, glossy black traveler crosses impossible worlds, each governed by one rule everyone else has learned to accept. The Dot asks the questions others have stopped asking. Every book is a complete adventure, with a larger mystery beneath the crossings.

For curious readers who love impossible places and questions that change everything.

  • Standalone adventures
  • One rule per world
  • A connected mystery

Readers: Curious readers · all ages welcome

Middle-grade adventure

Journey

The road did not end.

At ten, Sam Calder finds a mile marker beyond the end of the map. One road leads to forgotten towns, mountain crossings, and a larger network. Sam grows up a year at a time, learning that discovery is only the beginning of responsibility.

For readers who love maps, grounded adventure, and a hero who grows with the story.

  • Read in order
  • One year per book
  • A growing map

Readers: Ages 8+

Follow the Journey ↗journey.i4seer.com

Middle-grade horror

Seer Frights

You should be scared.

Ordinary kids run into bad rules, dangerous places, and everyday things that stop pretending to be harmless. The stories are standalone, fast-moving, creepy and funny—with real scares, real laughs, and no gore.

For readers who want fast chapters, real scares, and one more page before lights out.

  • Standalone scares
  • Fast chapters
  • Creepy & funny

Readers: Ages 8+

Enter Seer Frights ↗seerfrights.com

Adult literary horror

Seer Warns

You were warned.

In Harmon, a stranger sits down across from ordinary people and calmly tells them the shape of what is coming. They believe the warning. They make the choice anyway. Each novel stands alone; the town, its people, and the consequences connect them.

For readers who want character-driven dread, small-town secrets, and difficult choices.

  • Standalone novels
  • One shared town
  • Human consequences

Readers: Adult

Enter Harmon ↗seerwarns.com

Dark fae horror

Seer Fairies

Every fairy is real. The rules were forgotten.

Ancient fae followed their people across the sea. Generations later, the old bargains still bind—but the rules have been forgotten. The Seer follows the waking, one American town and one complete folklore mystery at a time.

For readers who want uneasy folklore, forgotten agreements, and field notes that make the familiar strange.

  • Standalone case files
  • Dark folklore
  • Field notes in every book

Readers: Upper middle-grade · lower YA

Standalone fiction

Seer Stories

Small details. Lasting consequences.

A missing case. A letter that should never have arrived. A room that does not add up. Historical mysteries, suspense, quiet horror, and lives changed by the details people notice. Every novel introduces its own setting and cast.

For readers drawn to everyday mysteries, skilled work, and choices that stay with you.

  • Complete standalone novels
  • Read in any order
  • Mystery, history & suspense

Readers: Teen & adult
