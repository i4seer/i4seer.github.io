Seer Frights #1 — Swamp Glow

Parts List

Everything you need. Where to find it.

Field notes — Mrs. Calhoun's supply list, reconstructed. Everything below is what you need to build the Swamp Glow.

VERSION 1 — THE EASY ONE

Green LED, diffused, 5mm

Qty: 1

The soft kind — not the bright clear ones. Diffused LEDs scatter the light so the whole puck glows evenly. About $0.25 each.

Amazon → Mouser →

CR2032 coin battery

Qty: 1

The flat silver one. 3V. Available at any drugstore, grocery store, or online. About $0.50 each. OR use 1 AAA battery — softer glow, bigger housing needed.

Amazon →

Slide switch, mini

Qty: 1

Optional. Tiny on/off switch so you don't have to pull the battery out. Skip this if you want to keep it simple.

Amazon →

Electrical tape

1 roll

Black electrical tape. Holds the battery and LED legs together. Any hardware store or dollar store.

Amazon →

Clear plastic ornament ball

Qty: 1

The kind that splits in half. Golf-ball size for CR2032, tennis-ball size for AAA. Craft stores carry them year-round.

Amazon →

Aquarium silicone

1 tube

Optional. Seals the ball seam for waterproofing. Skip if you're not putting it in water. Must be 100% silicone, no anti-mildew additives.

Amazon →

Green glass paint

1 bottle

Optional. One coat makes the clear ball look swampy. Without it, the LED still glows green through the clear plastic.

Amazon →

Fine sandpaper

1 sheet

Optional. 220-grit or higher. Roughing up the ball frosts the surface and scatters the glow. Works without it, but looks better frosted.

Amazon →

VERSION 2 — ADD THESE

Building Version 2? You still need everything above, plus these extra parts. Requires soldering. Buy extras of both Version 1 and Version 2 parts — in case you want to build more or need to replace one that didn’t work out.

CMOS 7555 timer IC

Qty: 1

ICM7555 or TLC555, 8-pin DIP package. The low-power version of the classic 555. About $0.50 each.

Amazon → Mouser →

Resistor, 330Ω

Qty: 1

LED current limiter. 1/4 watt. Orange-orange-brown color bands.

Amazon → Mouser →

Resistor, 10kΩ

Qty: 1

R1 in the timer circuit. 1/4 watt. Brown-black-orange color bands.

Amazon → Mouser →

Resistor, 2.2MΩ

Qty: 1

R2 in the timer circuit. Sets the slow pulse timing. 1/4 watt. Red-red-green color bands.

Amazon → Mouser →

Electrolytic capacitor, 470µF

Qty: 1

Timing capacitor. 16V or higher rated. The stripe marks the negative side. Watch polarity.

Amazon → Mouser →

Perfboard, small

Qty: 1

Small piece of prototyping board with holes. Solder the timer circuit onto this. Cut to fit your housing.

Amazon →

2× AAA battery holder

Qty: 1

Holds 2 AAA batteries in series for 3V total. Replaces the CR2032 from Version 1. With wire leads.

Amazon →

Hookup wire + solder

1 spool ea.

22 AWG solid-core hookup wire. Rosin-core solder. You’ll also need a soldering iron (not included — borrow one).

Amazon →

VERSION 3 — GO FURTHER

Building Version 3? No soldering. No timer chip. Just the ATOM Matrix, a cable, a battery pack, and the same housing from Versions 1 & 2.

ATOM Matrix

Qty: 1

24mm square, 7 grams. ESP32 processor, 5×5 RGB LED matrix, Wi-Fi, accelerometer. USB-C power. About $15.

Buy →

USB-C cable

Qty: 1

Powers the ATOM Matrix. Any USB-C cable will work.

Amazon →

Small waterproof power bank

Qty: 1

Or a long USB cable to a hidden outlet. Tuck the power bank in a waterproof bag and bury it near the roots.

Amazon →

Housing

—

Same as Versions 1 & 2 — plastic ornament ball, aquarium silicone, sandpaper, green glass paint. See above.
