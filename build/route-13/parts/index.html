Seer Frights #2 — Route 13

Parts List

Everything you need. Where to find it.

Field notes — the pressure pad supply list. Everything below is what you need to build the Route 13.

VERSION 1 — THE EASY ONE

Pressure mat switch

Qty: 1

The flat, flexible kind. Used in door chimes and Halloween props. Two thin strips that touch when you step on them. About the size of a welcome mat. Under $2 online.

Amazon →

Orange LED, diffused, 5mm

Qty: 1

The soft kind, not the bright clear ones. Diffused LEDs scatter the light for a warm glow. About $0.25 each.

Amazon → Mouser →

CR2032 coin battery

Qty: 1

The flat silver one. 3V. Available at any drugstore, grocery store, or online. About $0.50 each.

Amazon →

Electrical tape

1 roll

Black electrical tape. Holds the wiring connections together. Any hardware store or dollar store.

Amazon →

VERSION 2 — ADD THESE

Building Version 2? You still need the pressure mat and LED from above, plus these extra parts. Requires soldering. Buy extras of both Version 1 and Version 2 parts — in case you want to build more or need to replace one that didn’t work out.

555 timer IC

Qty: 1

The classic NE555, 8-pin DIP package. About $0.25 each. The chip that makes the LED pulse and fade.

Amazon → Mouser →

Resistor, 560kΩ

Qty: 1

Works with the capacitor to set the 30-second fade time. 1/4 watt. Green-blue-yellow color bands.

Amazon → Mouser →

Resistor, 100Ω

Qty: 1

Protects the LED from too much current. 1/4 watt. Brown-black-brown color bands.

Amazon → Mouser →

Resistor, 10kΩ

Qty: 1

Pull-up resistor for the trigger pin. 1/4 watt. Brown-black-orange color bands.

Amazon → Mouser →

Electrolytic capacitor, 100µF

Qty: 1

Sets the fade time. 16V or higher rated. The stripe marks the negative side. Watch polarity.

Amazon → Mouser →

2× AAA battery holder

Qty: 1

Holds 2 AAA batteries in series for 3V total. Replaces the CR2032 from Version 1. With wire leads.

Amazon →

Perfboard, small

Qty: 1

Small piece of prototyping board with holes. Solder the timer circuit onto this.

Amazon →

Hookup wire + solder

1 spool ea.

22 AWG solid-core hookup wire. Rosin-core solder. You’ll also need a soldering iron (not included — borrow one).

Amazon →

VERSION 3 — GO FURTHER

Building Version 3? You still need the pressure mat from Version 1. No soldering required for the ATOM Matrix itself — just two jumper wires and a resistor to the mat.

ATOM Matrix

Qty: 1

24mm square, 7 grams. ESP32 processor, 5×5 RGB LED matrix, Wi-Fi, accelerometer. USB-C power. About $15.

Buy →

Pressure mat switch

Qty: 1

Same as Versions 1 & 2. See above.

USB-C cable

Qty: 1

Powers the ATOM Matrix. Any USB-C cable will work.

Amazon →

USB power source

Qty: 1

Wall adapter, power bank, or long USB extension to a hidden outlet.

Amazon →

Jumper wires + 10KΩ resistor

2 wires + 1 resistor

To wire the pressure mat to the ATOM Matrix GPIO pin. Pull-up resistor connects GPIO 32 to 3.3V.

Amazon →
