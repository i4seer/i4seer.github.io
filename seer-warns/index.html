SEER WARNS

A stranger tells you exactly how your life falls apart. You believe every word. You do it anyway.

Seer Warns is our adult literary horror series. Standalone novels about ordinary people who receive warnings so precise they cannot be doubted — and walk into the fire anyway.

The Series

Twenty standalone literary horror novels. Each one follows an ordinary person. A teacher. A clerk. A contractor. A surgeon. Someone who receives a warning so precise it cannot be doubted.


They hear the prediction. They see the wall. They keep driving. Not from stupidity. Not from arrogance. Because the cost of stopping is its own kind of death. The slow kind, where you survive but stop being yourself.


Each book stands alone. Pick up any volume. You don’t need the others.

The Seer

The only recurring element across twenty books. Different face every time. Different place. Same calm. Same certainty. Same quiet delivery of a truth the protagonist already suspected but hadn’t yet named.


The Seer doesn’t cause anything. The Seer describes fires that are already burning.


What makes it horror isn’t the warning. It’s watching someone walk into a fire they can see, holding the hand of the person they love most, and understanding exactly why they can’t let go.

Seer Warns is published by Institute for Seer LLC. Literary horror for readers of Stephen King, Cormac McCarthy, Dennis Lehane, and Megan Abbott.


These are books about kitchens and mortgages and school pickups and the small, specific ways people destroy everything they’ve built.
