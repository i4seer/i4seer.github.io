The books.

Twenty novels, one small town. Each one is a self-contained warning. Read in any order. The cost of stopping is something the protagonists can’t pay either.

The Good Father

A history teacher. A promise to his mother. A spreadsheet that makes the math work. Until it doesn’t. A stranger at the diner tells him exactly how this ends. He places the bet anyway.

Seer Warns · Book One · Out now

The Honest Woman

Nineteen years in the basement office of the town hall. Twenty years of falsified water reports in a drawer she finally opens. Six hundred jobs hang on the plant her own family founded. A stranger names, in her own careful clerk’s voice, exactly what filing the truth will cost her. She files it anyway.

Seer Warns · Book Two · Out now

The Quiet Man

Haskell Hardware, the store Boyd’s father built in 1961. A pencil mark on the door frame, four-foot-eleven, in his dead son’s hand. When the big-box circular lands, the stranger names the August the store closes and the May his wife leaves. Closing it means closing the last room where Tyler existed.
